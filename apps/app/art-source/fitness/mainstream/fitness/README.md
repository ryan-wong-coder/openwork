# Chest, shoulder, arm and core originals

Ten exact catalogue movements have individually generated and reviewed artwork and
videos. Every `*-request.json` contains the final built-in Image Gen prompt (and any
targeted correction) plus the final video prompt. Only the established Zeno mascot
was an identity reference; no restricted exercise artwork was supplied or copied.

`*-generation.json` records the actual official LibTV CLI task/node, Seedance 2.0 VIP
model and settings. Eight-second 720p outputs were encoded to silent H.264/yuv420p,
1024 × 576, CRF26 with fast-start metadata. Posters use cwebp quality86. Complete final
hashes and format probes are in `provenance.json`.

Every eight-frame contact sheet was actually viewed. Some generated videos contain
two complete repetitions despite the prompt requesting one; each review records the
observed movement rather than claiming a prescribed cadence. The plank is an
isometric hold. No professional technique or clinical validation is claimed.

`rejected-attempts.json` records rejected variants and the initial lateral-raise
video. Its corrected second attempt starts at a near-straight-arm T pose and was
accepted only after reviewing the full down/up motion. Rejected videos were never
registered or published. Original generated video bytes were hashed and removed
after accepted encoding and frame review; final app files are under
`apps/app/public/zeno/fitness/`.

The `0313` hammer-curl Chinese step correction was checked against the primary
[ACE Hammer Curl instructions](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/10/hammer-curl/).
The source text's palm rotation contradicted its own English hammer-curl identity.
Only that exact record is corrected; frozen upstream files and other variants stay intact.
